# Assessment of the Proposed Mapping between Harmonic Lattice Parameters and D3-branes

**Date:** 26 August 2026  
**Status:** Independent evaluation of the supplied formulae

---

## Supplied Formulae

1. D3-brane mass
$$
M_{D3} = \frac{1}{(2\pi)^3 \alpha'^2 g_s} \cdot \left( \frac{R_{\rm lat}}{D} \right)^3
$$
Claimed result: $\approx 10^{12}$ eV.

2. Compact volume
$$
{\rm Vol}(S^5) = \pi^3 \left( \frac{R_{\rm lat}}{D} \right)^5 \alpha'^{5/2}
$$

3. String coupling
$$
g_s = \frac{1}{R_{\rm lat}} + \frac{1}{D}\alpha
$$
With $R_{\rm lat}=2^{18}$, $D=80$ this yields $g_s \approx 9.5 \times 10^{-5}$.

---

## Evaluation

### String coupling $g_s$
The expression $g_s = 1/R_{\rm lat} + \alpha/D$ has no basis in standard string theory or in the geometry of an FCC + C60 lattice. In type IIB string theory the string coupling is a modulus (or is fixed by fluxes / non-perturbative effects). Combining a pure lattice integer with the fine-structure constant in this additive way is an ad-hoc construction, not a derivation.

### D3-brane tension / mass
The standard D3-brane tension is
$$
T_3 = \frac{1}{(2\pi)^3 \alpha'^2 g_s}.
$$
Multiplying by a volume $(R_{\rm lat}/D)^3$ requires a precise identification of the lattice spacing with the string length $\alpha'$. No such identification is derived; $\alpha'$ remains a free scale. Consequently the numerical claim $M_{D3}\approx 10^{12}$ eV is not fixed by the lattice parameters alone.

### Compact volume
Writing ${\rm Vol}(S^5)$ in terms of $R_{\rm lat}/D$ again assumes an unproven equality between the lattice ratio and the AdS / sphere radius measured in string units. Without a dynamical mechanism that stabilises that radius, the expression is a definition, not a prediction.

### Claimed zero-error lepton masses
No explicit spectrum calculation (open-string modes on the D3-brane, Kaluza–Klein reduction, or otherwise) is supplied. The assertion that electron, muon and tau masses emerge with zero error therefore cannot be verified.

---

## Conclusion

The proposed mapping introduces several new relations that are not standard in string theory and are not uniquely determined by the C60 / FCC / E8 geometry previously defined. They function as additional free choices that can be adjusted to produce desired energy scales. As such they do not convert the earlier numerical constructions into a first-principles derivation.

A consistent embedding would require:
- a concrete compactification (metric, fluxes, stabilisation mechanism),
- a derivation of $g_s$ and of the compact radii from the lattice data without intermediate targets,
- an explicit open-string or Kaluza–Klein spectrum whose lowest modes reproduce the observed lepton masses.

None of these steps are present in the supplied text.
