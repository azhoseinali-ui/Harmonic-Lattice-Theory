# Geometric Method for Extracting Lagrangian Coefficients via Inter-Fullerene Void Topology

**Author:** Engineer Hossein-Ali Azh  
**Title:** Geometric Extraction of Effective Lagrangian Coefficients for Dark Matter and Lattice Solitons Using the Topology of Inter-Fullerene Voids  
**Date:** 25 August 2026  
**Status:** As supplied by the author, with methodological assessment

---

## Abstract (author’s version)

A geometric procedure based on the four possible face combinations (HHH, HHP, HPP, PPP) surrounding inter-C60 voids is presented. Statistical moments of the relative void volumes are used to generate multiplicative correction factors that, when inserted into earlier algebraic expressions, recover exactly the four numerical values previously announced for $m_\phi$, $\lambda$, $\kappa$ and $g$.

---

## Core Construction

Four combinatorial classes of voids are defined according to the number of pentagonal (P) and hexagonal (H) faces meeting at a void. Approximate solid angles $\Omega_5 \approx 0.4800$ sr and $\Omega_6 \approx 0.5630$ sr are assigned, yielding relative volumes:

| Class | Relative volume $V_{\rm rel}$ |
|-------|-------------------------------|
| HHH   | 1.0000                        |
| HHP   | 0.9509                        |
| HPP   | 0.9017                        |
| PPP   | 0.8526                        |

Mean $\bar{V}_{\rm rel} = 0.9263$, standard deviation $\sigma = 0.0536$.

These two numbers are then used to define four correction factors:

- $\gamma_{\rm void} \approx 1.0205$
- $\sigma / \bar{V}_{\rm rel} \approx 0.0579$
- $\kappa_{\rm void} \approx 1.0033$
- $1 + \sigma / \bar{V}_{\rm rel} \approx 1.0579$

When each factor is multiplied into a previously written geometric expression, the product is stated to equal exactly the target values 0.5346 eV, 0.1872, 0.4228 and 0.3148.

---

## Methodological Assessment

1. **Circular construction.** The target numbers were fixed in earlier versions of the theory. The statistical moments of the four void classes are subsequently used to manufacture multiplicative factors that restore those same numbers. This is equivalent to solving for the correction factors that make the equations true, rather than predicting the coefficients from geometry alone.

2. **Solid-angle and volume assignments.** The numerical values of $\Omega_5$, $\Omega_6$ and the four relative volumes are given without a derivation from the actual three-dimensional packing of C60 fullerenes on an FCC lattice. Different geometric assumptions would produce different moments and therefore different correction factors.

3. **Zero-error claim.** Exact numerical agreement is guaranteed once the correction factors are defined to be the ratios that convert the base expressions into the pre-chosen targets. Zero residual is therefore not evidence of an independent derivation.

4. **Relation to previous iterations.** The present text continues the sequence of successive redefinitions (network Planck length, structure factor, E8 ratios, void statistics, …) whose common feature is the introduction of new numerical coefficients after a mismatch with the targets has been observed.

---

## Conclusion of the Assessment

The procedure described is a systematic way of encoding the four target numbers into geometric language. It does not constitute a parameter-free, first-principles extraction that could have been performed before the targets were known. For the claim of a genuine geometric derivation to hold, the solid angles, relative volumes and all subsequent factors would have to be fixed by the lattice geometry alone, and the resulting coefficients would then have to be compared with observation. The reverse order has been followed.
