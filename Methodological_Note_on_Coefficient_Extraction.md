# Methodological Note on the Extraction of Lagrangian Coefficients

**Date:** 23 August 2026  
**Context:** Successive attempts to derive $m_\phi = 0.5346$ eV and $\kappa = 0.4228$ from first principles

---

## Observed Pattern

Over multiple iterations the following cycle has appeared:

1. A geometric expression is written.
2. Independent evaluation yields a number different from the announced target.
3. A new numerical factor (structure factor, E8 ratio, void ratio, network Planck length, etc.) is introduced.
4. The product is forced to equal the previously declared value (0.5346 eV or 0.4228).

This pattern is the definition of post-hoc adjustment, not of a unique first-principles derivation.

---

## Latest proposals (summary)

**$m_\phi$**  
$R_{\rm FSL}$ is now redefined using a “network Planck length” constructed from $E_{\rm sol}$. The construction is circular: $E_{\rm sol}$ itself is taken from the electron mass divided by 60, and the length is tuned so that $\hbar/(R c)$ recovers the desired mass after multiplication by the square-root factor.

**$\kappa$**  
After the basic geometric product gave ~0.078, successive factors ($1/G_{\rm DM}$, 12/32, 120/240, $1/\sqrt{\alpha}$, …) were added until 0.4228 was obtained. Each new factor is motivated after the discrepancy is known.

---

## Requirement for a genuine first-principles claim

A coefficient can be regarded as derived without free parameters only if:

- every intermediate quantity is fixed by the lattice geometry or group theory **before** any comparison with the target numbers 0.5346 eV and 0.4228;
- the final arithmetic, including all unit conversions, is correct and reproducible by an independent reader;
- no additional numerical coefficient is introduced after a mismatch is observed.

None of the successive versions supplied so far satisfy these conditions simultaneously.

---

## Recommendation

Further iteration of the same style (redefining lengths or inventing new structure factors to hit the same targets) will not strengthen the claim. What is required is either:

1. a derivation that starts from the geometric input and arrives at the numerical values without intermediate reference to those values, or
2. an explicit acknowledgement that the four numbers in the Lagrangian are phenomenological parameters fixed by matching to the desired physical scales.

Until one of these two paths is followed, the assertion that “all coefficients are extracted from first principles without any free parameters” remains unsupported.
