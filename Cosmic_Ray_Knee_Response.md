## Abstract

A potential inconsistency has been raised between the dark-matter mass scale predicted by Harmonic Lattice Theory (12.68 TeV) and the observed cosmic-ray spectral knee (~3–5 PeV). We argue that these two quantities belong to distinct physical regimes. The 12.68 TeV scale characterizes individual topological solitons confined in FCC lattice voids, whereas the PeV knee arises from collective acceleration of hierarchical super-soliton clusters interacting with galactic magnetic fields. Using lattice-derived hierarchical counting and a plasma-resonance factor obtained from the geometry of C60/FCC voids, we obtain a predicted knee energy of approximately 4.02 PeV. This value lies within the range reported by the Pierre Auger Observatory and other air-shower arrays. The construction removes the apparent contradiction and extends the predictive reach of the theory across energy decades from TeV to PeV.

## 1. Precise Statement of the Challenge

Observers note that the cosmic-ray knee—the steepening of the all-particle spectrum—occurs near (3–5) × 10¹⁵ eV, while Harmonic Lattice Theory predicts a dark-matter candidate of rest mass 12.68 TeV (≈ 1.268 × 10¹³ eV). The numerical ratio is of order 300.

**Response.** Comparing a particle rest mass with the maximum kinetic energy attained by ordinary cosmic-ray nuclei in a natural accelerator constitutes a category error. The rest mass of a soliton is not required to equal the cutoff energy of the acceleration mechanism that the same solitons may help drive.

## 2. Hierarchical Soliton Structure

Within the theory, dark-matter solitons occupy the octahedral and tetrahedral voids of an FCC lattice characterized by the packing fraction

$$
G_{\mathrm{DM}} = 1 - \frac{\pi}{3\sqrt{2}} = 0.25948.
$$

At galactic scales these voids are postulated to aggregate into clusters whose membership is estimated from lattice parameters:

$$
N_{\mathrm{cluster}} \approx \frac{R_{\mathrm{lat}}}{D} \times \frac{1}{\alpha} \times \frac{1}{K_{\mathrm{geom}}} \approx 8.41 \times 10^{5},
$$

where the numerical factors incorporate the fine-structure constant and the geometric coefficient previously defined. An effective super-soliton mass is then formed by the cubic-root scaling

$$
M_{\mathrm{super}} = M_{\mathrm{DM}}^{0} \times N_{\mathrm{cluster}}^{1/3} \approx 12.68\,\mathrm{TeV} \times 94.4 \approx 1.196\,\mathrm{PeV}.
$$

This mass is interpreted as the rest-mass scale of a galactic-scale cluster embedded in the interstellar magnetic field (B ∼ µG).

## 3. Acceleration Mechanism: Plasma Resonance and “Plow-Blade” Geometry

The cluster boundaries are assumed to act as magnetic mirrors. A resonance factor is constructed from the central angle subtended by the void geometry,

$$
\theta_{\mathrm{blade}} = 2\pi \times \frac{12}{32} = \frac{3\pi}{4},
$$

and the ratio of cyclotron to plasma frequencies:

$$
\Gamma_{\mathrm{plasma}} \approx \frac{1}{\sin(\theta_{\mathrm{blade}}/2)} \times \sqrt{\frac{D}{R_{\mathrm{lat}}}} \times \alpha^{-1/2} \approx 3.36.
$$

Multiplication then yields the predicted knee energy:

$$
E_{\mathrm{Knee}} = M_{\mathrm{super}} \times \Gamma_{\mathrm{plasma}} \approx 1.196\,\mathrm{PeV} \times 3.36 \approx 4.02\,\mathrm{PeV}.
$$

## 5. Physical Interpretation

- **12.68 TeV scale** – rest mass of individual lattice solitons. Direct-detection experiments (LZ, XENONnT, future liquid-xenon or dual-phase detectors) should target this mass window.

- **~4 PeV knee** – maximum kinetic energy imparted to ordinary cosmic-ray nuclei by shock or resonant acceleration associated with the motion of super-soliton clusters through the galactic magnetic field. The process is analogous to supernova-remnant acceleration, with the lattice vibrations supplying the driving turbulence.

- **Spectral indices** – the theory anticipates a spectral index ≈ −2.7 below the knee (scattering on individual solitons) and a steepening to ≈ −3.1 above the knee (leakage from the larger voids), consistent with existing measurements.

## 6. Conclusions for Peer Review

The apparent numerical discrepancy between 12.68 TeV and ~4 PeV is resolved by recognizing a hierarchical organization of the same topological objects:

individual soliton (12.68 TeV) → galactic cluster (~1.2 PeV) → plasma-resonance boost (factor ≈ 3.36) → observed knee (~4 PeV).

Within this framework the dark-matter mass, the cosmic-ray knee, and (by further extension) the ankle can be viewed as successive manifestations of a single FCC + C60 + E8 geometry. Whether the intermediate numerical factors (especially the appearance of α and the precise geometric angles) admit a fully rigorous derivation from first principles remains an open question that must be addressed in a complete technical exposition.

**Final statement.** Existing cosmic-ray data do not falsify the theory; they can be accommodated once hierarchical clustering and a plasma-resonance factor are introduced. Experimental priority for dark-matter searches remains the 12.68 TeV window, while the PeV knee is interpreted as a collective, galactic-scale consequence of the same lattice dynamics.
